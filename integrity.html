Academic Integrity Policy

This course strictly adheres to the academic standards and disciplinary regulations outlined in the official Nanjing University Student Handbook (南京大学学生手册). Students are expected to maintain the highest level of academic honesty, rigor, and independent scholarship. Academic misconduct fundamentally undermines the university's educational mission and will be handled strictly according to university protocols.

Examinations and Assessments

All midterms and final examinations must be completed strictly independently. In accordance with the university's examination regulations:

  • Unauthorized Materials: Bringing unauthorized notes, reference materials, or electronic devices (including smartphones and smartwatches) into the examination venue is strictly prohibited and constitutes cheating.
  • Misconduct During Exams: Looking at another student's paper, communicating during the exam, or disregarding the invigilator's instructions are punishable offenses.
  • Severe Violations: Taking an examination on behalf of another person (substitute test-taking), having someone else take an examination for you, or organizing coordinated cheating are classified as severe disciplinary violations that can lead to immediate expulsion.

Coursework and Assignments

Mathematics is a collaborative field, and you are encouraged to discuss course material and general problem-solving strategies with your peers. However, the boundary between collaboration and academic misconduct must be respected:

  • Independent Writing: After discussing a problem, you must write out your final proofs and solutions entirely on your own.
  • Plagiarism and Copying: Copying homework solutions from classmates, previous years' materials, online math forums, or unassigned textbooks is considered plagiarism (抄袭).
  • Ghostwriting and Unauthorized Tools: Submitting work completed by others, paying for assignment completion, or utilizing unauthorized generative AI tools to produce proofs are severe violations of academic norms.
  • Attribution: If your proof relies on a specific theorem or lemma outside of the official course lecture notes, you must explicitly cite your source.

University Disciplinary Sanctions

Violations of academic integrity will directly impact your standing in this course (resulting in a zero for the assignment or a failing grade for the course) and will be formally reported to the School of Mathematics and the university administration.

Depending on the nature and severity of the offense, Nanjing University imposes the following escalating administrative penalties (违纪处分):

  1. Warning (警告)
  2. Serious Warning (严重警告)
  3. Demerit (记过)
  4. Disciplinary Probation (留校察看)
  5. Expulsion from the University (开除学籍)
