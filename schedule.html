Weekly schedule

OverviewLecture notesAssignmentsScheduleAcademic integrity
01

Week 1

Line integrals and path independence. Differential form formulation of classical calculus theorems. Foundations of exterior algebra.

02

Week 2

Differential forms in Euclidean space. Exterior derivative operator and pullback map. Poincaré Lemma.

03

Week 3

Chain complexes and introduction to homological algebra. Definition and simple calculation examples of de Rham cohomology for open subsets of Euclidean space.

04

Week 4

Definition and classical examples of differentiable manifolds. Smooth maps, local coordinate transformations, and atlases.

05

Week 5

Submanifolds. Immersions, embeddings, submersions. Inverse function theorem, implicit function theorem, and the regular value theorem (preimage theorem).

06

Week 6

Tangent spaces and tangent maps. Smooth vector fields and their integral curves.

07

Week 7

Differential forms on manifolds and their operations. de Rham cohomology of manifolds. Volume forms and orientation of manifolds.

08

Week 8

Integration of differential forms on manifolds. Manifolds with boundary, induced orientation. Stokes’ theorem and its simple applications.

09

Week 9

Chain maps and chain homotopies. Homotopy invariance of de Rham cohomology and its applications.

10

Week 10

Long exact sequences in cohomology. Derivation of the Mayer-Vietoris sequence and its geometric applications.

11

Week 11

Poincaré duality theorem and its applications. Further calculations and applications of de Rham cohomology of manifolds.

12

Week 12

Definition of mapping degree and its topological significance. Applications of mapping degree.

13

Week 13

Index of isolated zeros of vector fields and the Poincaré-Hopf theorem. Introduction to Morse theory.

14

Week 14

Introduction to vector bundles and their classification problem. Differential geometry of vector bundles: curvature and connections. Characteristic classes of complex vector bundles.

15

Week 15

Characteristic classes of oriented real vector bundles, Euler class. Cohomology calculations of projective spaces and Grassmannians.

16

Week 16

Thom isomorphism. General Gauss-Bonnet-Chern theorem. Course summary.
