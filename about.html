About

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Mathematics at Nanjing University. Previously, I was a Limited Term Assistant Professor at the University of Georgia and an Assistant Professor (RTPC) at the University of Southern California. I received my Ph.D. in mathematics from Stony Brook University in 2018.

My research is in symplectic and noncommutative geometry, with a focus on Floer theory and Fukaya categories. See my research page for papers and current projects.
